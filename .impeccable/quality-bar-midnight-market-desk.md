# Midnight Market Desk — Quality Bar

This direction extends BursaIQ from an ask-first assistant into a role-aware exchange intelligence desk without losing its governed conversational core.

- **Purpose:** open with Today’s Brief so changed signals, pending decisions, and accountable next actions are visible before a user asks a question.
- **Story:** investigate a signal, keep its evidence attached, and move it into monitoring, Decision Memory, human verification, or a management-ready narrative.
- **Composition:** compact line-led ledgers; no grid of interchangeable cards. The fixed rail, fluid work area, and collapsible Decision Canvas remain the spatial model.
- **Materials:** midnight navy planes and blue-gray hairlines. Amber is scarce action and decisive value; teal explains analytical state, focus, and health.
- **Typography:** compact but readable operational type. Explanatory text stays at 10px or above, working body copy at 11px or above, and density comes from spacing rather than miniature labels.
- **Interaction:** every control reaches a 44px target; actions are reversible or explicit; Decision Canvas tabs remain keyboard complete; motion explains drawer, plot, and canvas state.
- **Access and truth:** role gates remain visible, synthetic data is labelled at point of use, local persistence is stated, and prototype workflows do not imply external notification.
- **Responsive finish:** Today’s Brief remains the first viewport on desktop and mobile; the navigation becomes an inert, dismissible drawer and ledger columns stack without hiding ownership or evidence.
- **Don’t** turn alerts, sources, or decisions into decorative floating cards.
- **Don’t** use amber and teal interchangeably, shrink metadata below legibility, or hide freshness and ownership behind a secondary interaction.
