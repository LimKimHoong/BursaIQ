# Apple-style Exchange Command Center — Quality Bar

This code-led direction translates the installed `apple-design` skill into BursaIQ’s operating context.

- **Purpose:** one obvious first action—ask BursaIQ—before any advanced controls.
- **Agency:** role-aware navigation, reversible drawers, visible evidence, and explicit verification states.
- **Materials:** midnight-indigo ground; heavier translucent navigation and sheets; lighter interactive surfaces; no ornamental glass stacking.
- **Typography:** SF Pro/system optical sizing, negative tracking only at display scale, comfortable body leading, and legible metadata.
- **Interaction:** immediate press feedback, 44px touch targets, spatially consistent right-side analysis, and no gratuitous bounce.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard focus, inert off-canvas background, Escape dismissal, meaningful status labels, reduced motion, reduced transparency, and increased contrast modes.
- **Finish test:** the first viewport should be remembered as a calm Bursa intelligence prompt—not as a generic chatbot or a crowded dashboard.
